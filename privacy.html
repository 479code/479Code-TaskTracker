Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

479Code Task Tracker ("the app") is an internal project and task management tool built by 479Code. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (encrypted, never visible to us), full name, and optional username, when you sign up.
  • Content you create: projects, tasks, subtasks, notes, and team membership data you enter into the app.
  • Usage data: timestamps of when tasks/notes are created or updated, for the purpose of showing activity within the app.

How it's used

Your data is used solely to operate the app: authenticating you, displaying your projects and tasks, enabling team collaboration features (invites, assignment, notifications), and nothing else. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database, secured with row-level access rules so that only members of a given project can see that project's data.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
